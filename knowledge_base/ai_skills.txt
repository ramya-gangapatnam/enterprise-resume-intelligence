Vector Databases:
Vector databases store embeddings for semantic search and retrieval. Examples include Pinecone, Weaviate, and ChromaDB.

LLMOps:
LLMOps involves monitoring, deployment, cost optimization, guardrails, and evaluation of LLM systems in production.

Prompt Engineering:
Designing structured prompts to reduce hallucinations and improve consistency.

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):
A system where relevant documents are retrieved before generating an answer to improve factual grounding.

Production AI Deployment:
Includes containerization, monitoring, logging, scaling, and latency optimization.
